## Product thesis

The approved About page describes three connected product ideas:

- Identity — reusable, candidate-controlled professional record
- Opportunity — structured, source-aware requirements
- Hiring — context crosses boundaries only through explicit actions

## Operating principles

The page preserves four explicit design constraints:

1. Candidate control
2. Evidence before opacity
3. System of record
4. Durable context

These are represented as product constraints rather than generic brand slogans.

## Deliberate exclusions

The approved page explicitly states that Relevyn is not:

- a social network
- a résumé marketplace
- an AI hiring oracle
- a replacement claim for every connected/external system

These exclusions are kept visible because they define product boundaries and prevent unsupported positioning.

## Non-claim boundary

The page intentionally does not invent:

- an origin story
- customer count
- growth claims

It explains product purpose and operating principles only.

## Responsive behavior

Operating principles move from a 2×2 grid to one column, exclusions move from four columns to two then one, and the closeout CTA stacks vertically.
