## Agreement ledger

The approved structure separates six responsibilities:

1. Account use
2. Candidate data
3. Organization use
4. Job + application data
5. External services
6. Acceptable use

## Responsibility lanes

The page explicitly separates the responsibilities of:

- Candidate — identity + sharing choices
- Employer — workspace + hiring process
- Relevyn — product boundary + platform operation
- External source — its own application/service flow

## Important source boundaries

The page preserves several product truths already established elsewhere:

- candidate private data remains candidate-controlled
- organization membership does not imply unrestricted candidate access
- jobs/applications retain source and workflow context
- external ATS/provider destinations retain their own terms where Relevyn does not own the flow
- acceptable use prohibits bypassing access boundaries or falsifying employment context

## Explicit legal boundary

The design explicitly states that final Terms must be reviewed by counsel before publication.

No commercial, liability, jurisdiction or other missing legal clauses are invented in this implementation.
