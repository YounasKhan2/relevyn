## Product privacy principles

### Data you provide

Candidate and organization data enters through explicit user/employer actions. Private Career sections remain candidate-controlled.

### Product-generated records

Applications, interview state, job records, provenance and operational metadata are generated through product workflows. Their authority depends on the workflow that created them.

### Sharing boundary

Applying creates the candidate-to-employer boundary. Organization membership alone does not unlock private Career data.

### External sources

Public jobs and company records can originate from employer systems or external providers. Source identity and freshness remain visible.

### Candidate controls

Resume-extracted information remains reviewable before approved facts enter the Career Passport.

## Explicit non-invention boundary

- subprocessors
